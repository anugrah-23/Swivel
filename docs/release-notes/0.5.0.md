# Swivel 0.5.0 — PDF to Word and PowerPoint

Drop a PDF on the wheel and three new wedges are waiting in **Convert**:

- **DOCX · Editable**: an editable Word document. With Microsoft Word installed, Swivel uses
  Word's own PDF reflow for the best result. Without Word, Swivel rebuilds the document itself:
  headings, paragraphs in reading order, pictures, and a new page for each PDF page. Complex
  layouts (columns, tables, text boxes) come across as plain flowing text.
- **PPTX · Exact**: one slide per page that looks exactly like the PDF. Perfect for presenting.
- **PPTX · Editable**: one slide per page with the text as real, editable text boxes over the
  page's pictures and shapes, in the original positions, sizes, colours and fonts.

Lecture PDFs exported from slides become widescreen decks; portrait documents keep their shape.
Everything still runs on your PC.

Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) have no text to edit: Word and PowerPoint get the pictures.

Swivel updates itself to this version within a day; or download the installer below.
